Key federal tax deadlines to keep on your radar. Contact us to discuss your specific filing situation.

  1. Jan 15

    Q4 estimated tax payment due (individuals & corporations)

  2. Jan 31

    W-2s and 1099s must be issued to recipients

  3. Mar 15

    S-Corporation and Partnership returns due (or extension)

  4. Apr 15

    Individual and C-Corporation returns due (or extension)

  5. Jun 16

    Q2 estimated tax payment due

  6. Sep 15

    Extended S-Corp, Partnership, and Q3 estimated taxes due

  7. Oct 15

    Extended individual returns due
