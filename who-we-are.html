Who We Are

An independent CPA and advisory firm building lifelong prosperity for Ohio families and businesses since 1933.

Our Firm

Flagel Huber Flagel is an independent certified public accounting and advisory firm with roots stretching back to 1933. With over 50 employees and more than 30 CPAs on staff, we are large enough to offer a full range of specialized services — and focused enough to know our clients personally.

We are not a national chain. We are a local firm with deep ties to the communities we serve across Cincinnati, Dayton, and Troy. Our advisors live here, our clients work here, and we are invested in this region's success.

Our tagline — Building Lifelong Prosperity — is more than a slogan. It reflects how we approach every relationship: not just for the next tax season, but for your entire financial life.

Our History

Nearly a century of service to Ohio businesses and families.

  1. 1933

    Firm founded in Dayton, Ohio during a time when businesses needed trustworthy guidance most.

  2. 1960s–80s

    Expanded services to include business advisory, audit, and specialized tax planning as clients grew.

  3. 1990s

    Added business valuation and ESOP advisory capabilities; expanded credentialed staff.

  4. 2000s

    Opened the Cincinnati office to serve the growing southwest Ohio market.

  5. Today

    Three offices, 50+ employees, 30+ CPAs — still locally owned and independent, still focused on building lifelong prosperity for our clients.
